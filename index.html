Personal Oura Analytics

Personal Oura Analytics is a private, non-commercial project intended to help its owner retrieve and review their own Oura sleep, recovery, heart-rate and activity measurements.

The planned application runs locally on the owner's computer. This website contains informational pages only; it does not collect Oura data or operate an OAuth callback.

Any Oura API connection will require the account holder to authorise access. Exported data may be shared separately with analysis services only if the account holder chooses to do so.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use.
