Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Personal Oura Analytics is designed solely for its owner's personal use. This public website is informational and does not contain login facilities or collect Oura health measurements.

Information accessed

If and when the separate local application is configured and authorised, it may retrieve Oura data within the scopes the owner grants, such as sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, stress and heart-health measurements.

Purpose and storage

The intended purpose is personal analysis and tracking. The local application is intended to save data on the owner's device, not publish it on this website. Credentials and health data should not be committed to a public GitHub repository.

Sharing

The planned application will not automatically transmit retrieved health data to third-party analytics or AI services. The owner may choose to upload selected exports to another service, subject to that service's separate privacy terms.

Control

The owner controls local data files and can revoke the application's permissions through Oura account controls. Revocation may not delete any local files already saved; those need to be deleted locally.

Changes

This policy will be revised if the app's actual data handling changes. It describes the planned private implementation, not a public service.
